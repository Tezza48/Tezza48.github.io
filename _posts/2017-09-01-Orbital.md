Orbital is a casual ball flinging, orbiting and point scoring game for mobile. it was created alongside _Phase Shift_. I took the role of Technical lead on the project as I had the most experience working with Unreal Engine 4 (the engine we originally intended to use for the game). After the initial concepting and designing of the game I had little input aside from the odd programming fix or workaround.

Orbital was created by:

- Aaron Mulligan: Design, Management
- Jamie Sams: Programming, Design
- Samuel Cook, Shane Stephens: Design/Concepting
- William Terry: Design/Concepting, Technical Management
