I feel like I have become the world's greatest hacker/detective - Pro enough to hack the Gibson... - in my attempts to set up Jekyll, install the relevant software to make Windows happy with it and trawling through individual files trying to find the cause of the mysterious invisible styling causing these pages to look like they were written in the early days of the internet.

Thanks for joining me and Hold on; it's gonna be a bumpy ride.
